Held-out test results · phishing classification

Phishing Triage Ollama MITRE ATT&CK
View code ↗

Parses .eml files safely (no URL is ever visited and attachments are only hashed in memory), extracts IOCs, and computes an explainable triage score from SPF/DKIM/DMARC, URL, attachment and language findings. A local Ollama model turns that evidence into a schema-validated report mapped to MITRE ATT&CK T1566, with email content treated as untrusted to resist prompt injection. Evaluated on 4,286 real phishing and 2,074 benign emails: 97.7% precision and 59.5% recall on a held-out test split.
