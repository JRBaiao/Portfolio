About Me

A detail-oriented, analytical student with a growing focus on blue-team cybersecurity and AI-driven solutions — grounded in collaboration, research, and innovation strategy.

Introduction

Hi, My name is João Rafael Avansini Baião, a technology enthusiast with a strong passion for Cybersecurity, Artificial Intelligence, and Cloud Computing.

I'm a graduate in Management of Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning from United Business Institutes, with a degree awarded by Middlesex University in London. I'm currently expanding my knowledge through a postgraduate program in Applied Artificial Intelligence at Erasmushogeschool Brussel.

My professional journey includes working as a Solutions Engineer Intern at Microsoft, where I gained hands-on experience with enterprise AI solutions, cloud technologies, workflow automation, and security-related projects. Alongside my professional experience, I've earned several industry-recognized certifications, including CompTIA Security+, CySA+, Network+, and SecAI+, plus two Microsoft certifications (see all credentials).

Beyond certifications and academic achievements, I enjoy turning theoretical knowledge into practical solutions. I've developed projects involving AI-powered threat detection, prompt injection analysis, cloud security, identity and access management, and security automation. I'm particularly interested in exploring the intersection of AI and cybersecurity, understanding emerging threats, and building technologies that are both innovative and secure.

What drives me most is curiosity. I believe technology is a field where learning never truly ends, and I'm constantly challenging myself to explore new programming languages, experiment with emerging technologies, and deepen my understanding of how complex systems work.

My goal is to contribute to building a safer and more intelligent digital world, combining technical expertise, creativity, and a continuous desire to learn.
